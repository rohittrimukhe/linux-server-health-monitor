Alerts, reports and the dashboard

Live alertsEvery minute for CPU, memory, disks and services. Email, Slack, Teams, webhook or syslog. Quiet hours, minimum duration, different recipients per area, acknowledge, and a daily digest.
ReportsDaily, weekly and monthly (summary and comprehensive) as HTML with hover charts, PDF, Excel and CSV. A database report for the DBA team. The customer logo and the server name, IP address and role are in every title.
DashboardLive CPU, memory, disks and network; all servers together; reports; alerts; settings; audit log; backups of the settings. Sign in with Linux accounts; customer accounts see only their own servers.
HTTPS certificateEncrypted by default with an automatically created self-signed certificate. Optional genuine free Let's Encrypt certificate, renewed automatically (needs a public DNS name).
Health of the toolLRS Vitals checks itself: watcher running, timers installed, reports on time, email delivered, disk space.
